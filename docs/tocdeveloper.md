## Facilities <a id="Facilities"></a>

### Trac <a id="Trac"></a>

If you want to send us patches for code review create a ticket here in Trac.

In case of problems with your Trac registration write to the [smartmontools team list](https://listi.jpberlin.de/mailman/listinfo/smartmontools-devel). This is a private list. Postings from non-member addresses are held in moderation queue and need manual approvement to go to the list. So you have to take a delay into account until your mail reaches the smartmontools team members.

### Mailing Lists <a id="MailingLists"></a>

#### smartmontools-devel <a id="smartmontools-devel"></a>

Private list for [team communication and orga](https://listi.jpberlin.de/mailman/listinfo/smartmontools-devel).

Web-Archives (members only)
- [JPBerlin](https://listi.jpberlin.de/mailman/private/smartmontools-devel/) (New List, started 2017-07-17)
- [Sourceforge](https://sourceforge.net/p/smartmontools/mailman/smartmontools-devel/) (Old List)

We use it only rarely for
- discussion of general strategy or questions
- announcements concerning release planning
- news and announcements concerning our projects IT infrastructure 

Maintainers of smartmontool packages are welcome to join ~ :)

Inquiry @Packagers: How can we make life easier for you? What are [your wishes](packagerswishlist.md)?


**@ALL** ![exclamation.png](assets/exclamation.png) If you see a failure or have a problem with our project facilities you may [report it here](mailto:smartmontools-devel@listi.jpberlin.de). You don't need to be subscribed for that. Your mail will then go to the list moderator and she will take action to solve the issue.

#### smartmontools-database <a id="smartmontools-database"></a>

List for [contributions of info about drives](https://listi.jpberlin.de/mailman/listinfo/smartmontools-database) that are not yet in smartmontools drive db.

Web-Archives (public)
- [JPBerlin](https://listi.jpberlin.de/pipermail/smartmontools-database/) (New List, started 2017-07-30)
- [Sourceforge](https://sourceforge.net/p/smartmontools/mailman/smartmontools-database/) (Old List)

Here we also collect info about [USB devices that have been successfully or unsuccessfully tested with smartmontools](supported-usb-devices.md). If you have a device not listed there, please tell us the test result, by editing the wiki page, and/or by sending an email to the [smartmontools-database mailinglist](https://listi.jpberlin.de/mailman/listinfo/smartmontools-database).

## Utilities contributed by Smartmontools users <a id="UtilitiescontributedbySmartmontoolsusers"></a>

From time to time it happens, that users contribute small utilities they wrote for additional tasks and analysis
in the context of SMART disk management. 

We place them on page [user contributed utilities](contributedutilities.md) at your own disposal.
We give no warranty for these, please contact the author directly, if you have any questions!
It is possible to give feedback or contribute enhancements by editing the page.
